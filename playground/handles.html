Kinetic² handles

A funscript as a composite cubic Bézier in the time-position plane: the knots on the author's clock, one angle and two handle lengths per knot, and the ceilings as bounds on the lengths.

the planner playground · github.com/openvalence/Kinetic

 

    Knot

    Click a knot. Drag a handle to set its angle and length, Shift-drag for the length only, drag the knot to move it.

    Ceilings and window

    The script's 0 to 100 maps onto the window; the window sits on the rail. A bigger window asks more speed of the same script.

    0 is PCHIP: crests and hold edges G1 as drawn, the corner ramp takes their acceleration step, through points G2 by their angle. 1 takes Makima's angles and makes crests and hold edges G2 by their lengths. Between, the free angles and lengths of the two blend.
